# Privacy Policy for Rafiq Al-Muslim (Muslim Companion)

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026  
**Developer:** Muslim Partner Studio

Rafiq Al-Muslim (Muslim Companion) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android application (`com.muslimpartnerstudio.rafiqalmuslim`).

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not require you to create an account or submit personal registration details to use the core features of the app, including prayer times, Quran reading, daily Adhkar, Tasbih, and Qibla direction.

## 2. Location Data (If Used)
If you grant location permission to calculate accurate local prayer times or Qibla direction, location data is used on your device to provide those features and is not sold or shared with third parties. You can manage or revoke permissions anytime in your device settings.

## 3. Local Storage & Preferences
App settings such as language preference (Arabic/English), dark theme settings, bookmarks, and reading or Adhkar progress are stored locally on your device.

## 4. Third-Party App Stores
When you download or update the app through third-party platforms or app stores, those platforms may collect technical or usage data according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Muslim Partner Studio through your official developer contact email.
